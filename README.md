# State of Blockchain & Web3 in Zambia (2022–2026)

A report on the growth of Zambia's blockchain and Bitcoin ecosystem — the communities that built it, the tools and platforms that power it, and the merchants putting it to real use.

Prepared by the **Blockchain and Web3 Community Network (BWCN)**.

📄 [Read the full report](bwcn-2026-report.md)

---

## About

Zambia's blockchain and Bitcoin ecosystem has grown from a handful of enthusiasts in 2021 into one of the most active, organised grassroots digital asset communities in Southern Africa — built by communities and educators, not institutions or government policy.

This report documents that growth across four parts:

1. **Grassroots Blockchain and Bitcoin Communities (2022–2026)** — 17 organisations, from Bitcoin Zambia and BitDevs Zambia to university and developer-focused hubs
2. **Service Providers, Tools, and Infrastructure** — the exchanges, wallets, and platforms making digital assets accessible to everyday Zambians
3. **Merchant Adoption and Circular Economies** — verified Bitcoin-accepting businesses across Zambia
4. **Observations and the Road Ahead** — what the data shows, and what needs to happen next

## Why This Report

Zambia's ecosystem has been under-documented relative to its actual activity. This report exists to:

- Give funders, partners, and media an accurate, current picture of the space
- Help communities coordinate rather than operate in silos
- Support Zambia's case as an emerging African blockchain development hub
- Create a citable, updatable public record of the ecosystem's growth
